## Q1: [Financial Compliance] For Overseas Chinese: What documents do foreigners need to work in China?

**A**: Foreigners working in China need a work permit and work-type residence permit. Process: 1) Employer applies for Foreigner Work Permit Notice; 2) Foreigner applies for Z visa at Chinese embassy; 3) Within 30 days of entry, obtain Work Permit; 4) Apply for residence permit.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国刑法》

**领域**: 刑事辩护 | **语言**: English

## Q2: For Students Abroad: What legal risks should overseas Chinese be aware of when investing in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese investing in China should be aware of: 1) Foreign Investment Negative List restrictions; 2) Foreign exchange controls requiring compliance approval for profit repatriation; 3) Tax compliance considering China tax law and bilateral tax treaties. We recommend engaging professional lawyers for comprehensive legal due diligence before investment.

**法律依据**: 《外商投资法》

**领域**: 涉外投资 | **语言**: English
